## 2. Six-Scene Demonstration Sequence

### Scene 1: System Overview & 5 Core Nodes
1. **Navigate to**: `Overview` (Home) or `Devices` (`/devices`).
2. **Key Talking Points**:
   - Exactly five logical edge nodes are configured representing the baseline industrial workcell:
     - **Node 1**: Arduino Nano 33 BLE Sense (IMU gesture classification).
     - **Node 2**: ESP32 + MAX9814 (MFCC acoustic anomaly detection).
     - **Node 3**: ESP32 + ADXL345 (3.2 kHz bearing vibration harmonic extraction).
     - **Node 4**: ESP32 + PIR/ToF (Perimeter safety verification - Awaiting integration).
     - **Node 5**: ESP32 + BME680 (Environmental drift compensation - Awaiting integration).
   - Point out the top simulation banner:
     *"Simulated Edge AI Fleet: Telemetry values, FFT features, and INT8 scores are generated by the local simulator. Hardware is currently unattached."*
   - Verify KPI cards (Fleet Health, Pipeline Throughput, Anomaly Rate) reflect the continuous simulated stream.

---

### Scene 2: Explainable Inference & Live Inspection
1. **Action**: In the `Devices` page, locate **Node 3 (ESP32 Vibration Node)** or **Node 2 (ESP32 Acoustic Node)**.
2. **Action**: Click the card or the **Analyze** button (`ArrowUpRight` icon).
3. **Key Talking Points**:
   - The **Node Live Detail Inspector Modal** opens without full page reload.
   - Live stream chart shows 1-second sample cadence with bounded history buffer.
   - Active TinyML Prediction: e.g., `Normal Steady State` ($92\%$ confidence).
   - Natural Explainable AI reasoning strip:
     *"Model evaluates steady-state continuous signals without anomalous statistical drift."*
   - Transparent disclosure: Model inference rules and FFT spectral features are synthesized in the local simulation engine.

---

### Scene 3: Fault Injection & Hardware-Aware Gateway Failover
1. **Navigate to**: `Dynamic Scheduling` in the sidebar (`/dynamic-scheduling`).
2. **Action**: Review the current policy selector (*Fault-Tolerant High-Availability*).
3. **Action**: Click **Inject Node 3 Fault (Test Failover)** (`Zap` icon).
4. **Key Talking Points & Transitions**:
   - Node 3 triggers an SPI Overrun condition.
   - The scheduler dynamically recalculates assignments.
   - **Hardware Compatibility Audit**:
     - The scheduler checks peer capability matrix `NODE_CAPABILITIES`.
     - *Why not Arduino Nano 33 BLE?* While it has an onboard LSM9DS1 IMU ($952\text{ Hz}$), it has **no physical wiring access** to Node 3's external motor-mounted ADXL345 and insufficient sample rate ($952\text{ Hz} < 3200\text{ Hz}$).
     - The scheduler rejects Nano 33 BLE and routes `task-vib-fft` directly to **Raspberry Pi 5 Gateway (Host Failover)**.
   - Review the event log entry:
     *"ALERT: Node 3 SPI Overrun! Verified edge peer constraints... Autonomously migrated task-vib-fft to Raspberry Pi 5 Gateway."*
   - Latency and energy trade-offs are clearly marked as `est. ms` and `est. µJ`.
5. **Action**: Click **Clear Fault & Restore Task** (`RotateCcw` icon).
   - The workload restores immediately to `node-esp32-adxl345`.

---

### Scene 4: Decentralized Federated Learning (FedAvg)
1. **Navigate to**: `Federated Learning` in the sidebar (`/federated-learning`).
2. **Key Talking Points**:
   - Illustrates simulated on-device FedAvg model training with zero raw data leakage.
   - Formula prominently displayed: $\mathbf{w}_{\text{global}} = \sum_{k} \frac{n_k}{N_{\text{total}}} \mathbf{w}_k$.
   - Three clients participating with real sensor sample sizes:
     - Node 1: $150$ samples ($\Delta w: \pm 2.4\%$)
     - Node 2: $220$ samples ($\Delta w: \pm 2.6\%$)
     - Node 3: $310$ samples ($\Delta w: \pm 2.1\%$)
3. **Action**: Click **Execute FedAvg Round** (`Play` icon).
   - Aggregates client parameter vectors using sample count weights into the global layer vector.
   - Round counter advances to Round 4 / 5.
   - Accuracy increases to simulated $92\%$; cross-entropy loss decreases to $0.24$.
   - Auditable parameter table appends Round 4 record showing the exact global layer vector.
4. **Action**: Click **Reset** (`RotateCcw` icon) to demonstrate baseline restoration back to Round 1 / 5 (`v1.1.0`).

---

### Scene 5: Compiler Benchmarking & Honest CSV Export
1. **Navigate to**: `Engine Benchmarks` in the sidebar (`/benchmarks`).
2. **Key Talking Points**:
   - Compares **TensorFlow Lite Micro (INT8)**, **Edge Impulse EON**, and **ARM CMSIS-NN**.
   - Transparent Provenance Disclosure:
     *"Benchmark Data Provenance: Values shown are classified as `Configured Demo Fixture` grounded in official MCU documentation. No live hardware silicon benchmarking is fabricated."*
3. **Action**: Click between the metric tabs:
   - **Latency**: Shows CMSIS-NN leads at $7.2\text{ ms}$.
   - **Memory**: Shows Flash ($32.6\text{ KB}$) and RAM footprints ($8.4\text{ KB}$).
   - **Energy**: Shows CMSIS-NN consumes only $28.4\ \mu\text{J}$.
4. **Action**: Click **Export Benchmark CSV** (`Download` icon).
   - Downloads `edgesphere_tinyml_benchmarks.csv`.
   - Open or inspect the CSV to show each engine record, MCU target, metrics, and `"Configured Demo Fixture"` classification.

---

### Scene 6: Recovery, Pause/Resume & Persistence
1. **Navigate to**: `Devices` (`/devices`).
2. **Action**: Click **Pause Stream** on the simulation banner.
   - Stream pauses; time-series ticks stop incrementing.
3. **Action**: Click **Resume Stream**.
   - Stream resumes smooth live generation.
4. **Action**: Refresh the browser (`F5` or `Ctrl+R`).
   - Versioned local storage (`v1`) loads persisted state cleanly.
   - Fleet initializes with 5 nodes, clean KPIs, and uncorrupted buffers.
